Questions

Which calculation method does it use?

The one you choose: Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, ISNA, Egyptian, Karachi, Ja'fari, Tehran and more, with the Asr madhab and a manual adjustment of each prayer.

Is it free?

It's $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year. If you truly can't pay, you can promise it by Allah in the app and use everything free for a year.

Is Ask Deen a mufti?

No. It gives general knowledge with its sources so you can learn and check. For personal rulings and sensitive matters, ask a qualified scholar.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Prayer times, the Qibla, the adhan, the Qur'an text, tasbih and your journal need no connection. Ask Deen, listening to recitations and the Arena do.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Spanish, Arabic and Dutch, with Arabic fully right-to-left.

Does it support Sunni and Shia Muslims?

Yes for prayer times (including Ja'fari and Tehran methods) and for the school Ask Deen follows. The hadith library currently has Sunni collections only.

What happens to my Ask Deen questions?

They're sent to an AI provider to be answered, without your name or account details, and kept in your history on your own phone. See the privacy policy for the details.

Why are there no ads?

The subscription pays for the app, so it never has to sell your attention or your data.

How do I cancel?

In your Google Play or App Store account, under Subscriptions. The app also has a shortcut in Settings → Subscription.

Where is the app?

It's coming to Google Play and the App Store. Meanwhile, Android users can install it from the download page.
