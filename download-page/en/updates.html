Updates and news

Every version of Better Deen, newest first. Updates reach the app through the download page and, once published, the stores.

2026-11-10 Update Version 0.17.21

Skins with their own shape and trail, and a full-screen shop

  • Skins now change shape too: flames, leaf fronds, clouds, wings, a crescent moon, icicles, a rising sun, spikes, a crown and a hood.
  • Every skin leaves its own trail behind: flames, sparks, stars, petals, snow, bubbles, embers, smoke or a galaxy. The fancier ones show even when you are not pressing.
  • Skins have a rarity, from Common to Legendary, shown on a bigger card.
  • The shop takes the whole screen, with tabs for skins, coins, packs, boosts and codes.
2026-11-10 Update Version 0.17.20

Skins with their own designs, and Noor Apparel

  • Every Salah Quest skin now has its own design: flames, filigree, mosaic, moon phases, roses, frost crystals, a setting sun, gold geometry, arabesques and a galaxy.
  • The Noor skin is now a carpet made after the Noor Apparel hoodie: heather grey, black side panels and the wordmark.
2026-11-10 Update Version 0.17.19

More skins, packs and a profile that is yours

  • 11 new Salah Quest skins, from Emerald Oasis to the 100,000-coin Celestial Star.
  • Coin packs (1, 10 and 100 hours of play) and skin packs in the shop, with the full price shown on every button.
  • The Noor hoodie bought on this website comes with a code that unlocks the Noor skin.
  • Customize your public profile: frame, background, title, skin and a short bio.
  • A new question asks, once, whether the games may send anonymous play data to improve them. You can say no and change it in Settings.
2026-11-10 Update Version 0.17.18

Fixes: start-up and widget taps

  • The app no longer stays on the green start screen when a start-up step is slow.
  • Widgets and the next-prayer bar always open the part of Home they are meant to.

  • The app can count, anonymously, which screens are used so we know what to improve. Never your name, prayers or anything you write, and you can turn it off in Settings.
