Terms of use

Last updated: 10 November 2026

These terms apply when you use Better Deen (“the app”). By using it you accept them. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

The app

Better Deen helps you practise Islam: prayer times and reminders, the Qur'an, a knowledge assistant, tools and a game. We work to keep it accurate and available, but we can't guarantee that it will always work without interruption or errors.

Subscription

  • The app is paid. You can subscribe monthly or yearly through Google Play or the App Store. The price is the one the store shows before you confirm.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before, in your store account settings. Payment is charged to your store account.
  • Refunds follow the rules of the store you bought from (Google Play or Apple).
  • Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel a subscription.

If you can't pay

If you truly can't afford the subscription, you can promise it in the app and use it free for a year, renewable. This is an act of good faith between you and Allah. We may change or end this option in the future; if we do, it won't affect a year already granted.

Religious content

  • Prayer times are calculated from your location and the method you choose. Check them with your local mosque, especially in high latitudes.
  • Ask Deen is an AI assistant: it gives general knowledge with its sources and may make mistakes. It is not a fatwa. For personal rulings and sensitive matters, consult a qualified scholar.
  • Qur'an texts and translations come from recognised sources named in the app. Translations convey meanings and are not the Qur'an itself.

The Arena

The Arena is an optional competition. Play fairly: modifying the app, automating answers or using several accounts to gain an advantage is not allowed and may lead to removal from the rankings and loss of prizes. Player names must be respectful. Prizes are governed by the Arena rules.

Acceptable use

Don't use the app or its services to break the law, harm others, attempt to access other people's data, or overload or interfere with our servers. We may suspend accounts that do.

Your content and ours

Your data is yours; see the privacy policy. The app's design, code and texts written by us belong to Better Deen. Qur'an texts, translations, recitations, sounds and fonts belong to their owners and are used under their licences, which are listed in the app.

Coins, skins and codes

Coins, skins, frames, titles and the other items of the games are virtual items for fun. They have no cash value, can't be exchanged for money or transferred, and are only for your use in the app. Coins are earned by playing; you can also buy some with real money through Google Play or the App Store. These purchases are optional, change only the look of the game or save time, and never make a Competitive or ranked run easier.

Purchases are final unless the store's refund rules or the law of your country say otherwise; refunds are asked for from the store. If you are under the age at which you can buy in your country, ask a parent or guardian first. A redeem code works once, for one account, and may not be sold.

We may change prices, rebalance how many coins the games give, and retire or replace items to keep the games fair, but we won't take away an item you paid for without a refund.

Game data

To improve the quality of the games we ask your permission to collect anonymous play data (see the privacy policy, “Game data and quality”). You can say no, or change your mind in Settings, and the games work the same. Cheating, tampering with the app or sending false scores to a ranking can lead to your scores being removed and your profile being excluded from the rankings and prizes.
