Last updated: 6 October 2026

The app

Better Deen helps you practise Islam: prayer times and reminders, the Qur'an, a knowledge assistant, tools and a game. We work to keep it accurate and available, but we can't guarantee that it will always work without interruption or errors.

Subscription and free trial

  • The app is paid. You can subscribe monthly or yearly through Google Play or the App Store. The price is the one the store shows before you confirm.
  • New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. If you cancel before it ends, you aren't charged.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before, in your store account settings. Payment is charged to your store account.
  • Refunds follow the rules of the store you bought from (Google Play or Apple).
  • Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel a subscription.

If you can't pay

If you truly can't afford the subscription, you can promise it in the app and use it free for a year, renewable. This is an act of good faith between you and Allah. We may change or end this option in the future; if we do, it won't affect a year already granted.

Religious content

  • Prayer times are calculated from your location and the method you choose. Check them with your local mosque, especially in high latitudes.
  • Ask Deen is an AI assistant: it gives general knowledge with its sources and may make mistakes. It is not a fatwa. For personal rulings and sensitive matters, consult a qualified scholar.
  • Qur'an texts and translations come from recognised sources named in the app. Translations convey meanings and are not the Qur'an itself.
